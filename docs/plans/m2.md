# M2 plan: geospatial value

Status: accepted 2026-10-25, with every recommendation below taken. Scope from `../spec/01-spec.md` §10: spatial and temporal search, map
UI, raster and vector derivatives, TiTiler previews.

## Where M1 left us

Already done, and not repeated here: `bbox`/`intersects` search with antimeridian handling,
keyset paging (SRCH-1, SRCH-2), the MapLibre search tab with footprints, header extraction for
GeoTIFF/COG, LAS/LAZ, GLB and GeoParquet, and upload status by polling.

What M2 actually has to build:

- **Temporal search means acquisition time.** Today `datetime` filters on `created_at`, the
  upload time. STAC's `datetime` is when the data was captured; nothing extracts that yet.
- **Vector files have no reader.** GeoPackage, Shapefile and GeoJSON are classified as
  `vector` but get no footprint or CRS; only GeoParquet does.
- **Nothing is derived.** There is a `derived` container in the ports and Terraform, but no
  outbox, no assets table, no derive worker, and ING-7 is marked M3.
- **No byte leaves through the API.** Items have no downloadable assets, thumbnails or tiles,
  so every new byte path in M2 is new access-control surface.

## Decisions

All eight were settled as recommended; the slices below assume them.

| # | Question | Recommendation | Why |
|---|----------|----------------|-----|
| D-M2-1 | How are tiles authorized? TiTiler has no idea who may read what. | **Our own tiler service** (titiler.core as a library, our auth and `authz` SQL, reading COGs through the blob port). An audited `POST /items/{id}/preview` mints a short-lived preview token scoped to one item; tile requests carry it and are not audited one by one. | A SAS URL handed to stock TiTiler would leak in query strings and bypass ACC-1. Auditing every tile would flood `audit.events` (a map view is hundreds of tiles). Needs an ACC-5 amendment: "a preview session is audited when it opens". |
| D-M2-2 | Footprint vector tiles: Martin, or our own? | **Our own MVT endpoint** (`ST_AsMVT` over the same filtered search query). | Martin reads Postgres directly and can't apply `authz.readable_scopes` to the caller. Our endpoint reuses the search store, so SRCH-1 and ACC-1 hold by construction. |
| D-M2-3 | pgSTAC and titiler-pgstac mosaics? | **Not in M2.** Single-item previews only; mosaics (and spike S3) wait for a need. | Mosaics are the only reason for pgSTAC (spec D7). Single-item previews cover "see what this raster is". |
| D-M2-4 | Should `datetime` change meaning? | **Yes, to acquisition time** (STAC semantics), with a new `created` filter for upload time. Items with no acquisition time don't match a `datetime` filter. | Matches STAC and what users mean by "imagery from 2023"; the web app is the only client. |
| D-M2-5 | Where do derive jobs run? | **A separate stage**: the outcome transaction writes an outbox row; a relay publishes it to a `derive` queue; the same worker image runs a `derive` entrypoint. Size classes S/M only (meshes are M3). | Cataloging stays fast (the < 60 s time-to-searchable target), a failed derivative never touches the item's outcome (ING-2), and big jobs scale separately. |
| D-M2-6 | Downloads: stream through the API, or redirect to a read SAS? | **Audited redirect** to a read-only SAS for one blob, ≤ 15 min. | Keeps the API off the byte path, as uploads do; ACC-8 extends to read URLs. |
| D-M2-7 | LIF-1 (legal hold) is tagged M2, but retention policies are M5. | **Move LIF-1 to M5**, with retention. | Nothing in M2 deletes or tiers originals; a legal-hold check with nothing to hold is untestable. |
| D-M2-8 | Live status: SSE now? | **Yes, in slice 7.** Fetch-streamed `text/event-stream` (EventSource can't send the bearer token), nginx buffering off, a bounded server loop. | Derivatives make processing take minutes; this is when polling stops being enough. |

## New and changed invariants

ING-7, ING-11, ING-12 and ACC-9 are in `../spec/03-invariants.md` as planned; the amendments
land with the slice that enforces them:

- **ING-7 Derivatives are rebuildable** (moved from M3): deleting every derivative and
  rerunning derive yields equivalent assets (same roles, media types and lineage; checksums
  equal where the recipe is deterministic).
- **ING-11 Derivation never changes an outcome.** A derive job only adds assets to a cataloged
  item; it never alters the item row's identity, marking or outcome, and never runs on anything
  quarantined or rejected (extends ING-4).
- **ING-12 Exactly one asset per role and recipe.** Redelivered or crashed derive jobs leave one
  asset per `(item, role, recipe_version)`, and the asset row and its blob agree (extends ING-1
  and ING-5 to `derived/`).
- **ACC-9 Every byte path asks the same question.** Downloads, thumbnails, preview tokens, tiles
  and footprint tiles serve only items the caller may read under `authz.readable_scopes`; an
  unreadable item answers exactly like a missing one (ACC-1 and ACC-2 extended to bytes).
- **ACC-8 amended**: read URLs are scoped to one blob, read-only, and expire within 15 minutes.
- **ACC-5 amended** per D-M2-1: opening a preview session is the audited event for its tiles.
- **SRCH-1** gains the acquisition-time and `created` filters; its simulator model gains them.

## Slices

Each slice is one PR, ends with `make check`, and adds its mutants. Slices 1 and 2 are
independent and can run in parallel; 3 to 6 follow 2.

### 1. Acquisition time and the temporal filter (SRCH-1)

- Readers report capture time where the header has one: TIFF `DateTime`/GDAL `ACQUISITIONDATETIME`,
  LAS file creation day/year, GeoParquet/`geo` metadata where present.
- Migration: `catalog.items.datetime_range tstzrange` with a GiST index; the STAC Item shows
  `datetime`, or `start_datetime`/`end_datetime` for a range, and `datetime: null` otherwise.
- `datetime` filters on overlap with `datetime_range`; a new `created` filter takes over upload
  time. The search simulator's model and the web Filters panel follow (labels "Captured" and
  "Uploaded").
- Manifest expectations for capture time on the samples that have one, taken from an
  independent source (gdalinfo, lasinfo), never from our readers.

### 2. Vector footprints (ING-6)

- Readers for GeoPackage (`gpkg_contents` extents and `gpkg_spatial_ref_sys`, read through the
  blob port's range reads under the extraction budget) and Shapefile (bbox in the 100-byte
  header).
- Shapefile CRS lives in a `.prj` sidecar. Until bundles exist, a lone `.shp` gets a bbox
  with no CRS and therefore no footprint. Decide in the PR whether a zipped shapefile counts.
- GeoJSON has no header: footprint from a bounded streaming scan (ING-6's fallback path),
  skipped over a size cap, with `extraction: partial`.
- Samples and manifest entries for each, with independent expectations (ogrinfo).

### 3. Derive framework: outbox, assets, worker (ING-7, ING-11, ING-12)

- Migration: `ingest.outbox` (written in the outcome transaction), `catalog.assets` (item, role,
  media type, blob name in `derived/`, size, sha256, `processing:` lineage, recipe version),
  `ingest.derive_jobs` keyed by `(item_id, role, recipe_version)`.
- An outbox relay (in the event shim locally, a small daemon in cloud) publishes to a `derive`
  queue. Queue port and contract tests gain the second queue.
- `worker/derive.py`: a step machine like `pipeline.py` (claim, render to bounded local
  scratch, upload with "refuse to overwrite", record the asset, all idempotent) behind a
  `Recipe` protocol, one per (kind, role).
- Items gain STAC `assets`: the original plus each derivative, with roles and no hrefs yet.
- `test_derive_sim.py`: the derive machine over a faulty queue and store, with crash-after-N
  replay, a "delete all derivatives and rerun" step for ING-7, and checks for ING-11 and ING-12.
- Mutants: overwriting an existing derivative, deriving a quarantined item, recording the
  asset before its blob is written.

### 4. Raster derivatives

- Recipes: `cog` (GDAL's COG driver: overviews, internal tiling, deflate; skipped when the
  original already is a valid COG, and the original's role says so) and `thumbnail` (PNG,
  ≤ 512 px, from the overviews).
- Bounded scratch per size class; jobs over the class limit wait for M3's L class.
- `make image-smoke` covers the GDAL drivers the recipes use.

### 5. Byte paths: downloads and thumbnails (ACC-8, ACC-9)

- `GET /items/{id}/assets/{role}`: audited, answers 404 for unreadable items like
  `/items/{id}`, then 307s to a read SAS for exactly that blob (D-M2-6).
- The signer port gains read URLs; `test_signed_urls.py` proves Azurite enforces read-only and
  the expiry. The catalog simulator gains a download actor.
- Results and item details show thumbnails, through the same route.

### 6. Previews and footprint tiles (ACC-9, ACC-5)

- `tiler/`: a FastAPI service (its own composition root, built by `wiring.py`) using
  titiler.core's tile factory over COG assets, reading through the blob store port.
- `POST /items/{id}/preview` (API, audited) returns a preview token (signed, item-scoped,
  ≤ 15 min) and the tiler's TileJSON URL. The tiler checks the token and nothing else.
- `GET /tiles/footprints/{z}/{x}/{y}.mvt` (API) takes the search filters as query parameters,
  runs the search store's query through `ST_AsMVT`, and audits once per map view (a
  `footprints.view` event keyed by its filters), not per tile. That needs a short ACC-5
  wording change, made with D-M2-1.
- nginx routes `/tiles/`; compose and Terraform gain the tiler (Container Apps, same image).
- A tiles simulator, or rules in the search simulator: tiles never contain an unreadable item,
  and a preview token for one item never serves another.

### 7. Live status over SSE (D-M2-8)

- `GET /uploads/events`: the caller's own uploads, streamed as stage changes (from the outbox
  and jobs tables, polled server-side within a bounded loop and a maximum stream lifetime, then
  the client reconnects with `Last-Event-ID`). One audit event per stream opened.
- Stages extend to `deriving`, and an upload reports `ready` once its derivatives settle.
- nginx: `proxy_buffering off`, read timeout above the stream lifetime. The web app reads the
  stream with `fetch` and falls back to M1's polling on error.

### 8. Map UI

- Toggle the preview layer for a selected raster; fit to its footprint; opacity control.
- At low zoom, show every match as a footprint tile instead of the current page's GeoJSON. The
  result list still pages.
- Thumbnails in the result list and details; asset downloads in details, each with its marking.
- "Captured" and "Uploaded" date filters (slice 1); derivative status in Upload and details.

### 9. Close-out

- Manifest: expected derivatives per sample (roles, media types; COG validity checked by an
  independent validator, not our recipe).
- `make check`, `make explore`, `make mutants`, `make soak`, `make e2e`; report status for
  ING-7, ING-11, ING-12, ACC-8, ACC-9, SRCH-1 and SRCH-2.
- README status and the spec's milestone table.

## Not in M2

Mosaics and pgSTAC (D-M2-3), point cloud COPC and 3D Tiles (M3), mesh anything (M3), retention
and legal hold (M5, D-M2-7), an upload cancel endpoint (needs the pipeline's per-blob lock;
could slot into slice 3 if wanted), adaptive block sizing and client-side checksums.

## Risks

- **GDAL writing through the blob port.** Reads go through the port; COG writing needs local
  scratch and an upload. Scratch has to be bounded and cleaned on crash (`CrashAfter` covers the
  upload half, not the disk).
- **Tile latency.** Every tile reads COG ranges through the port; a per-process range cache in
  the tiler is likely needed to stay usable. Measure before building it.
- **GeoJSON footprints** need a full read; a size cap means big GeoJSON files get no footprint
  until a derive step computes one.
